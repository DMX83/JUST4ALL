Privacy

Just4Folders runs on your Mac. No telemetry, no account and no server of our own.

What data we handle

  • Files and folders: only the ones you grant. They are read and processed locally and are never uploaded.
  • Preferences: stored in the app’s UserDefaults, inside its container.
  • Search index: SQLite in ~/Library/Application Support/JUST4FOLDERS.
  • AI key: if you enable AI, it is stored in the macOS Keychain. It is never written to disk or sent to third parties.

Optional AI

If you enable AI, Just4Folders sends the provider you choose (for example DeepSeek) truncated text and minimal metadata to classify a file or expand a search. It never sends complete files. Without a key, the app works with local rules only.

macOS permissions

The app may ask for access to Desktop, Documents, Downloads and external volumes. It does so to list and organize those locations; you can revoke the permission in System Settings.

Donations

Donations are handled on the web (Buy Me a Coffee) and are subject to that platform’s policy. There are no purchase or donation links inside the app.
